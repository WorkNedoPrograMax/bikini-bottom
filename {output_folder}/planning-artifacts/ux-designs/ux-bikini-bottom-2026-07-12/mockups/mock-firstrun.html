welcome aboard · first run
Mr. KrabsAhoy, Maksym — welcome to Bikini Bottom! This here's a little town where me crew does yer work for ye: hand out an order, and a neighbour gets cookin'. When somebody finishes or needs ye, they'll call out — ye can't miss a thing.
Mr. KrabsWhere's the work? Pick yer project folder — the one folder the whole town works on. Can't open the town without it!I'll set up me kitchen in there meself — you touch nothin' technical.
Mr. KrabsGot yer crew's pictures and voices? Drop 'em in later whenever ye like — the town works fine with stand-ins till then.
Mr. KrabsLast thing — can I holler at ye? When a crew member finishes an order or gets stuck, I'd like to send ye a wee note even when the town's behind other windows. Entirely up to you, mind.🔇 Prefer quiet? Ye can mute all sounds later — the notes and red dots still work.

First Run as a scene, not a settings form: Mr. Krabs welcomes you, needs your project folder (the one must-have), mentions crew pictures and voices can come later, and asks — but never demands — permission to notify you. Then the town opens.
